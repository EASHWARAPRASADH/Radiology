# 16 — Risk Register

Status: draft v0.1 — 2026-11-25 · Part of the Radio R&D blueprint (see 00-index.md)

**Scales.**

- **Likelihood (L):** 1 rare · 2 unlikely · 3 possible · 4 likely · 5 almost certain.
- **Impact (I):** 1 minimal · 2 minor · 3 moderate · 4 major · 5 severe.
- **Score = L×I.** ≥ 15 red, 8–14 amber, ≤ 7 green.

Scores are initial estimates, reviewed monthly. Patient-safety hazards are analysed in detail in the ISO 14971 table in 09-safety-validation.md. This register tracks programme-level risks and references those hazards.

| R26 | Technical / evidence | Public "official" test splits are not patient-disjoint, which inflates every reported metric. **Observed 2026-11-25:** 111 of the 139 ChestX-Det test images that can be mapped to NIH patients share a patient with ChestX-Det train (73 patients). The other 414 test images can't be checked | 5 | 4 | 20 | Fingerprint-map public sets to source patient IDs (`radio dedupe-chestx-det`, `leak-check-chestx-det`). Gate only on leak-reduced splits (`regate-detector`). Report leaky-only metrics alongside to show the inflation. Never cite public-split numbers as performance evidence. Re-split by patient once full NIH metadata is mapped | ML lead | Leaky-only metrics clearly above leak-reduced metrics |
| R27 | Technical | Saliency from the Phase-1 ViT is not anatomically meaningful. **Observed:** activation-map peaks hit radiologist boxes at chance rate (mean 0.11 vs 0.10 chance). For effusion they fall below chance (0.02 vs 0.10) because peaks sit on image borders and corners, consistent with DINOv2 high-norm artifact tokens (Darcet et al., ICLR 2024). This risks misleading overlays and hides shortcut learning | 5 | 3 | 15 | Activation maps stay `validated=false`, display-only, and are never used as measurement or laterality evidence. Use a validated detector for localisation. Switch the encoder to DINOv2-with-registers (`vit_*_reg4_dinov2`, Apache-2.0) and re-run `eval-pointing`. Mask border tokens; audit saliency before any UI display | CV lead | Pointing hit rate ≤ chance on the next encoder |
| R28 | Clinical / human factors | Uncertainty overload: drafts list many "possible" findings, so radiologists learn to ignore them (alert fatigue) or anchor on them (automation bias). **Observed 2026-11-25:** with the Phase-1 classifier, a mean of 5.0 of 5.5 asserted findings per ChestX-Det report are `uncertain` (13 §6.3) | 4 | 4 | 16 | Do not pilot until the uncertain share per report meets a target set in the SAP. Stronger perception model (full-data, higher-resolution training). Report the deferral burden alongside accuracy in P4. Reader-study probes for over- and under-reliance | ML lead + clinical lead | Mean uncertain findings per report above target on the P4 validation set |
| R29 | Technical | The term-based validator does not recognise findings named by synonyms. **Observed:** it caught 4% of injected synonym mentions ("pleural fluid", "volume loss") (13 §6.2) | 3 | 4 | 12 | Text the generator did not write never reaches a draft unvalidated: rephrased sentences face a closed vocabulary (0 of 46,132 hostile insertions survived). The validator is not used on free text such as radiologist reports. Any future free-text path needs an NLP finding extractor validated on its own | NLP lead | A new free-text path proposed into the draft |

## Top risks (score ≥ 15)

R02 (licensed data access), R26 (leaky public test splits, observed), R27 (meaningless ViT saliency, observed), R10 (shortcut learning), R11 (domain shift), R15 (FDA pathway for report drafting), R16 (EU NB / AI Act), R21 (competition), R01 (research-only data in shipped weights), R05 (false negatives + automation bias).
